# 🌻 Adrien's Farm

> *The land remembers everything. Adrien is learning to listen.*

Adrien's Farm is an AI-illustrated children's storybook about a child who discovers that the farm is not a place where things grow — it is a place that is alive. The story moves through the seasons with Adrien, introducing composting, mycelium networks, seed saving, water cycles, and reciprocal relationships with the soil as lived experiences rather than lessons.

This is a project about wonder as a methodology. About ecological literacy that begins in the body before it reaches the mind. About what it means to be in relationship with a living system rather than in control of it.

**Status:** Early-stage — story structure and illustration pipeline under active development · **Org:** [MelodicBloom](https://github.com/MelodicBloom)

---

## The Story So Far

Adrien is seven. The farm has been in the family for three generations but no one talks about it that way — it is just where they live, where the mornings smell like wet earth and the evenings sound like crickets and the kitchen always has something growing on the windowsill.

One afternoon, Adrien follows a beetle into the compost pile and discovers it is not a pile of rot. It is a city. From there, the story spirals outward: the mycelium network beneath the vegetable beds, the seed vault in the barn, the rain barrel that knows something about patience, the old apple tree that has been watching longer than anyone.

Each chapter introduces one ecological concept through one sensory encounter. No lectures. Only noticing.

## Illustration Pipeline

Illustrations are generated via a hybrid AI + human editorial process:

1. **Scene description** — written from Adrien's sensory perspective, not a camera angle
2. **Prompt assembly** — structured prompt pack with consistent character design, color temperature, and rendering style per chapter
3. **Generation** — AI image generation with multiple variations per scene
4. **Editorial curation** — human selection and annotation for story coherence
5. **Color consistency pass** — palette locked per season (spring: yellow-green-violet; summer: warm amber-green; autumn: ochre-rust-deep green; winter: blue-grey-white)

### Aesthetic Direction

Warm, imprecise, hand-drawn quality. Slightly overexposed light. Soft edges. The world should look like it is made of something you could touch. Influences: Shaun Tan's environmental intimacy, Beatrix Potter's material specificity, Isol's wry humor.

## Contributing

Adrien's Farm is a living project and community authorship is one of its core principles.

**Ways to contribute:**

- 🌱 **Write a scene** — open a PR to `story/chapters/` with a new sensory encounter. Keep it under 300 words. Stay in Adrien's body.
- 🎨 **Suggest an illustration** — open an Issue with a scene description and any visual references. Tag it `illustration-suggestion`.
- 🐞 **Add an ecological concept** — if there is a living system relationship you think belongs in Adrien's world, open an Issue tagged `story-concept`.
- 🌾 **Share farming knowledge** — if you grow things and want Adrien's Farm to get the details right, open an Issue tagged `fact-check`. Local, regional, and traditional ecological knowledge especially welcome.
- 🌍 **Translate** — this story belongs in every language. Open an Issue tagged `translation` with your language and we will figure out the workflow together.
